# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. This is a plain-language summary; the linked text is the standard we hold each other to.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps the project:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it.
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising, and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space where someone represents the project.
It also covers sharing private information about the deployment this project runs on, such as tokens, chat ids and
repository contents.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit or reject
contributions that do not follow them.

Report abuse privately to `samanhappy@gmail.com`. All complaints are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy
and safety of the reporter are respected. Enforcement steps follow the Contributor Covenant's guidance: correction,
warning, temporary ban, then permanent ban, chosen in proportion to the behaviour and its impact.
